Home Styling Tips

✨ The Mirror Trick

Place a large mirror opposite your window. It reflects natural light and makes any small room feel double its size instantly.

🕯️ Scent Layering

Combine diffusers with soy candles. A vanilla and jasmine scent creates a premium atmosphere for your guests.

🌿 Green Life

Add indoor plants like Snake Plants or Monstera. They purify the air and add a luxury "organic" look to your corners.
